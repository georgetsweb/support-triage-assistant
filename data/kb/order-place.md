# Placing an order

1. Add items to your basket.
2. Click the **basket** icon, then **Checkout**.
3. Sign in, create an account, or continue as a guest.
4. Enter or choose your delivery address and pick a delivery option.
5. Choose a payment method (see *Payment methods*) and click **Place order**.

You'll get an order confirmation email within a few minutes, with your **order number**. Keep it for any questions about the order.

**Can't complete checkout?** Make sure every required field is filled in, and that your billing address matches the one your bank has. If payment fails, see *Payment problems*.

Items in your basket aren't reserved until the order is placed, so popular items can sell out in the meantime.
