# Requesting a refund

1. Go to **My account > Orders**, open the order and click **Return items**. Guests can use **Help > Returns** with their order number and email.
2. Select the items and a reason. For damaged or faulty items, add a photo.
3. Print the prepaid return label, or show the QR code at a drop-off point.
4. Send the parcel within **14 days** of starting the return.

**When you'll get your money:** once the return reaches our warehouse (usually 3-5 business days after you send it), we check it within 2 business days and issue the refund. Your bank then takes **5-10 business days** to show it.

**Damaged on arrival?** You don't need to send it back first. Report it within 7 days of delivery with photos, and we'll refund or replace it straight away.

For conditions and what can be returned, see *Return and refund policy*. To check on a refund already in progress, see *Tracking a refund*.
