# Managing shipping addresses

**Saving an address**
Go to **My account > Addresses > Add address**. You can save up to 10 addresses and mark one as the **default**, which is pre-selected at checkout. You can also add a new address during checkout.

Please include a phone number; couriers may call if they can't find you.

**Changing the address for an order you've already placed**
- **Before it ships** (status "Processing"): open the order under **My account > Orders** and click **Change order**.
- **After it ships:** we can't change it any more. Use the courier's tracking link; most couriers let you redirect the parcel to another address or a pickup point, sometimes for a small fee.

**Moved house?** Update your default address in **My account > Addresses** so future orders go to the right place. Changing your saved address doesn't change orders that are already placed.

We deliver to all EU countries and to selected countries worldwide; checkout shows whether we deliver to your country.
