# Tracking your order

When your order ships, we email you a **tracking link**. You can also track it any time:
- Signed in: **My account > Orders**, then open the order.
- As a guest: **Help > Track order**, then enter your order number and email address.

**What the statuses mean:**
- **Processing:** we're preparing your order. You can still change or cancel it.
- **Shipped:** it's with the courier. Tracking updates may take up to 24 hours to appear.
- **Out for delivery:** it will arrive today.
- **Delivered:** the courier has marked it as delivered.

**Marked as delivered but not received?** Check with neighbours and any safe place, and wait until the end of the next business day; couriers sometimes mark parcels early. If it still hasn't arrived, contact us and we'll investigate with the courier.
