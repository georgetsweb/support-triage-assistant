# Switching account type

We offer four account types:

| Type | Price | Main benefits |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 0 | Order history, saved addresses |
| Standard | 4.99 EUR/month | Free standard delivery on all orders |
| Gold | 9.99 EUR/month | Free express delivery, early access to sales |
| Platinum | 19.99 EUR/month | Everything in Gold, plus a dedicated support line and extended 60-day returns |

**To switch:** go to **My account > Account type**, choose the new type and confirm.

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately. You pay the difference for the rest of the current month.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the start of your next billing month, so you keep your current benefits until then.

Leaving an annual plan early may involve a fee; see *Cancellation fees*.
