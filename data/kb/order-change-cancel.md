# Changing or cancelling an order

**Before the order ships** (status "Processing"), you can change or cancel it yourself, free of charge:
1. Go to **My account > Orders** and open the order.
2. Click **Change order** to edit items, quantities or the delivery address, or **Cancel order** to cancel it.

If you cancel, we refund the full amount, including delivery, to your original payment method within 5-10 business days.

**After the order has shipped**, it can no longer be changed or cancelled. Instead:
- Refuse the delivery, or
- Return the items once they arrive (see *Return and refund policy*).

Guests can manage their order with the link in the confirmation email.

Orders usually ship within 1 business day, so the sooner you act, the better.
