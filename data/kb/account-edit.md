# Updating your account details

You can change most details yourself at any time.

1. Sign in and go to **My account > Profile**.
2. Edit your name, phone number, email address or password.
3. Click **Save changes**.

**Changing your email address:** we send a confirmation link to the new address. The change only takes effect after you click it, so you can still sign in with the old address until then.

**Changing your password:** you'll need your current password. If you've forgotten it, use *Resetting your password* instead.

Saved delivery addresses are managed separately; see *Managing shipping addresses*.
