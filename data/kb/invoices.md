# Finding and downloading invoices

Every order gets an invoice, created when the order ships.

**To view or download it:**
1. Sign in and go to **My account > Orders**.
2. Open the order and click **Download invoice (PDF)**.

Guests can use the **View invoice** link in their shipping confirmation email.

**Business invoices:** to add a company name and VAT number, enter them at checkout. Invoices can't be reissued with a different company name after the order has shipped.

**Something wrong on your invoice?** Contact us within 30 days with the invoice number, and we'll check it and send a corrected invoice if needed.

If you paid for a paid account type, those invoices are under **My account > Account type > Billing history**.
