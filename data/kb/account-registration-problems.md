# Problems signing up

**I didn't get the confirmation email**
- Check your spam or junk folder.
- Wait 10 minutes; emails can be delayed at busy times.
- On the sign-in page, click **Resend confirmation**. Confirmation links expire after 24 hours.

**"This email is already registered"**
An account already exists with that address. Use **Forgot password** to get back in (see *Resetting your password*).

**The form won't accept my password**
Passwords need at least 8 characters, including one number.

**The page shows an error or keeps reloading**
Clear your browser cache, disable ad blockers for our site, or try another browser.

If none of this works, contact us (see *Contacting customer service*) with the email address you used and a screenshot of the error.
