# Tracking a refund

See the status of your return under **My account > Orders > Returns**, or with the tracking link in your return confirmation email.

**Return statuses:**
- **Return started:** waiting for your parcel.
- **In transit:** the courier has it.
- **Received:** it's at our warehouse and will be checked within 2 business days.
- **Refund issued:** we've sent the money. We email you when this happens.

**Refund issued but not in my account yet?** Banks take **5-10 business days** to show a refund. Card refunds sometimes appear as a separate credit rather than a cancelled charge.

If it's been more than 10 business days since "Refund issued", contact us with your order number and we'll send you the payment reference so your bank can trace it.

If your return has been "In transit" for more than 10 business days, contact us so we can check with the courier.
