# Resetting your password

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. Open the email we send and click the reset link. **The link is valid for 1 hour.**
4. Choose a new password (at least 8 characters, with one number).

For your security, we never ask for your password by email or phone, and our staff cannot see it.

**No reset email?** Check spam, wait a few minutes and try again. If you no longer have access to that email address, contact customer service. We'll verify your identity with your name, a recent order number and your delivery address before updating the email on your account.

After 5 failed sign-in attempts, your account is locked for 15 minutes.
