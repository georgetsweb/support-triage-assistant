# Delivery options

| Option | Cost | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Standard delivery | 4.99 EUR, **free on orders over 50 EUR** | Delivered to your door |
| Express delivery | 9.99 EUR | Order before 14:00 CET on a business day |
| Pickup point | 2.99 EUR | Collect from a partner shop or parcel locker near you |
| Click & collect | Free | Collect from one of our stores |

Standard members get free standard delivery, and Gold and Platinum members get free express delivery, on every order.

You choose the option at checkout. Some large or heavy items can only be sent by standard delivery; checkout shows this.

For how long each option takes, see *Delivery times*.
