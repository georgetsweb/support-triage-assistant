# Complaints and reviews

**Making a complaint**
We're sorry if something went wrong. To make a formal complaint, use **Help > Contact us** and choose **Complaint**. Include your order number and what happened; photos help for damaged items.
- You'll get a case number straight away.
- A team lead reviews every complaint and replies within **3 business days**.
- If you're not satisfied with the outcome, you can ask for it to be escalated to a manager.

**Leaving a review**
We'd love to hear about your experience.
- **Product reviews:** open the product page and click **Write a review**, or use the link in the email we send 7 days after delivery. You need to have bought the item.
- **Service feedback:** after any chat or call, you'll be asked to rate it from 1 to 5.

We publish all genuine reviews, positive or negative, as long as they follow our review guidelines (no personal data, no offensive language).
