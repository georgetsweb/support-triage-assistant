# Creating an account

You can shop as a guest, but an account lets you track orders, save addresses and download invoices.

**To create an account:**
1. Click **Sign up** at the top of any page.
2. Enter your name, email address and a password (at least 8 characters, with one number).
3. Choose an account type: **Free**, **Standard**, **Gold** or **Platinum**. You can change it later (see *Switching account type*).
4. Open the confirmation email we send you and click the link within 24 hours.

Your account is active as soon as you confirm your email. Only one account can be registered per email address.

If you don't receive the confirmation email or the form shows an error, see *Problems signing up*.
