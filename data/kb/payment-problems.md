# Payment problems

**My payment was declined**
- Check that the card number, expiry date and security code are correct.
- Make sure your billing address matches the one your bank has.
- Some banks ask you to approve online payments in their app; check for a notification.
- If it still fails, try another payment method, or ask your bank whether it blocked the payment.

**I was charged twice**
Usually one charge is only a **temporary hold** from a failed attempt. Holds disappear on their own within 3-5 business days. If both charges are still there after 5 business days, contact us with your order number and a screenshot of your statement, and we'll refund the duplicate.

**I was charged but have no order confirmation**
Check your spam folder and **My account > Orders**. If there's no order, the charge is a hold that will be released within 3-5 business days.

**The amount looks wrong**
Check whether the delivery cost or a currency conversion fee from your bank explains it. If not, contact us.
