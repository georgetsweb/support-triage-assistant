# Newsletter subscription

Our newsletter brings new products, offers and early access to sales, about once a week.

**To subscribe:** enter your email in the box at the bottom of any page, or tick **Send me the newsletter** under **My account > Communication preferences**. Confirm with the link we email you. New subscribers get a **10% welcome code**.

**To unsubscribe:** click **Unsubscribe** at the bottom of any newsletter, or untick the option in **My account > Communication preferences**. It takes effect within 48 hours.

**Not receiving it?** Check your spam folder and add our address to your contacts. If you unsubscribed in the past, you need to subscribe again.

Unsubscribing from the newsletter doesn't stop service emails about your orders, such as confirmations and tracking updates.
