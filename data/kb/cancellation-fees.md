# Cancellation fees

**Orders:** cancelling an order is free until it ships. See *Changing or cancelling an order*.

**Paid account types (Standard, Gold, Platinum):**

| Plan | Cancellation fee |
|---|---|
| Monthly | None. Your benefits continue to the end of the month you've paid for. |
| Annual, within the first 14 days | None. You get a full refund. |
| Annual, after 14 days | 20% of the price of the remaining full months |

**Example:** you cancel an annual Gold plan (119.88 EUR/year) with 6 full months left. The remaining value is 6 x 9.99 = 59.94 EUR. The fee is 20% of that, 11.99 EUR, and we refund the other 47.95 EUR.

You can see your exact fee before confirming, under **My account > Account type > Cancel plan**.
