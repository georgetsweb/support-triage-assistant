# Delivery times

| Option | Domestic | Within the EU | Rest of the world |
|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | 3-5 business days | 5-8 business days | 7-14 business days |
| Express | 1-2 business days | 2-4 business days | 3-6 business days |
| Pickup point / click & collect | 2-4 business days | Not available | Not available |

Times start when your order **ships**, not when you place it. Most orders ship within 1 business day; you get an email with a tracking link when yours does.

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. Delivery can take longer during sales and holidays.

If your order is more than **5 business days** late compared with these times, contact us and we'll open an investigation with the courier.
