# Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account at any time.

**Before you delete:**
- Wait until any open orders are delivered and any refunds are completed. Deletion is blocked while an order or refund is still in progress.
- Download any invoices you need (*Finding and downloading invoices*). They can't be recovered afterwards.
- Cancel any paid account type. Monthly plans end with no fee; annual plans may have one (see *Cancellation fees*).

**To delete:** go to **My account > Privacy > Delete account**, enter your password and confirm.

Deletion is final after **14 days**. If you sign in during those 14 days, the deletion is cancelled. After that, your personal data is erased, except records we must keep by law, such as invoices, which are stored securely for the legally required period.
