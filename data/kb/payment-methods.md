# Payment methods

We accept:
- **Credit and debit cards:** Visa, Mastercard and American Express
- **Digital wallets:** Apple Pay and Google Pay
- **PayPal**
- **Bank transfer:** your order ships once the payment arrives, usually within 1-2 business days
- **Pay later in 3 instalments:** for orders between 50 and 1,000 EUR, subject to approval
- **Gift cards** issued by our store, which can be combined with any other method

All prices are in **EUR**. If your card is in another currency, your bank converts the amount and may charge a fee.

Your card is charged when you place the order. Card details are handled by our payment provider and are never stored on our servers.

We don't accept cash on delivery or cheques.
