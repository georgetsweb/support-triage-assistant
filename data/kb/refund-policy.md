# Return and refund policy

You can return most items within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund. Platinum members get **60 days**.

**Conditions:**
- Items must be unused, in their original packaging, with tags attached.
- **Can't be returned:** opened hygiene products, personalised items, gift cards and digital downloads.
- **Faulty or damaged items** can always be returned within 30 days, even if opened, and we pay the return delivery.

**Return delivery:** free for faulty items and for Gold and Platinum members; otherwise 3.99 EUR is deducted from your refund.

**What's refunded:** the price of the items. The original delivery cost is refunded only if you return the whole order, or if the item was faulty.

Refunds always go back to the **original payment method**. To start a return, see *Requesting a refund*.
